# Changelog

## 0.1.0-alpha — unreleased

The custom ring surface of `@heliuslabs/zolana` moves into
`@heliuslabs/zolana-custom-rings`, built on `@heliuslabs/zolana` as a pinned
peer. `CustomRingClient` adds the ring proofs and the ring indexer reads to a
`ZolanaClient`. Custom rings keep members, lists, escrowed keys and mint
settings in Storage Program records that Ring RPC writes from signed record
updates, prove transfers on a confidential, an anonymous and a delegate rail,
and let an auditor open every slot under the layout the ring program verified.

Breaking

- `@heliuslabs/zolana/ring` and the custom ring, auditor and key registry
  exports of the `@heliuslabs/zolana` entry points move here → import them from
  `@heliuslabs/zolana-custom-rings` or its `client` and `keypair` entry points.
- `ZolanaClient`'s `proveCustomRing*`, `proveIndexedRingPolicy`, `proveIndexedRingDeposit`, `getRingSpendRecord`, `getRingKeyRegistryEntry` and `getRingKeyRegistryRegisterProof` are gone, `CustomRingClient.proveRing(request)` proves every ring circuit from a `RingProofRequest`, its `getStorageMemberOpenings`, `getStorageMemberRecords` and `getStorageRecordPool` read records from the ring indexer, and the rule table codecs, list writers, member predicates, `RegisterKeyStatement`, `auditSharedSecret`, the statements and the frame codecs move to `@heliuslabs/zolana-custom-rings/advanced` → create the client with `createCustomRingClient(config)`, implement `proveRing` in a custom `RingProver` and the three reads in a custom `RingRecordReader`, and import those helpers from the advanced subpath.
- `initializeRingInstruction` replaces `initializeRingConfigInstructions`, `createRingConfigInstruction`, `initSppRingConfigInstruction` and `createRingPolicyInstruction` and creates the ring's `RingPolicy` account, its optional policy over a records storage registry and the pool registration in one instruction the upgrade authority signs, naming the four `RingRoles` keys, `admin` for `updateRingAuthorityInstruction` and `setRingDelegateInstruction`, `policy` for policy writes and the authority requests Ring RPC accepts, `pauser` for `setRingPausedInstruction` and `cosignAdmin` for co-signer records, `updateRingPolicyInstruction` and `buildRingUpdatePolicyTransaction` replace `setRingPolicyRulesInstruction`, `setRingPolicySourceInstruction` and their transaction builders and also rebind the records, `updateRingAuthorityInstruction` replaces `setRingAuthorityInstruction` and hands over one `role` with the admin and the new key signing, `setRingDelegateInstruction` takes no payer and the delegate signs it, and each co-signer holds its own record that `setRingCoSignerInstruction` creates or rescopes without thresholds, `clearRingCoSignerInstruction` closes by its `signer` and `fetchRingCoSignerRecord` reads in place of `fetchRingCoSigner` → create the records registry first, pass four distinct nonzero `roles` and the table as `policy` to `initializeRingInstruction`, send each policy write as an `update` of kind `rules`, `source` or `records`, and appoint each co-signer with its own `setRingCoSignerInstruction`.
- `WalletRecordUpdate` and `AuthorityRecordUpdate`, sent with `RingRpc.submitRecordUpdate`, write a ring's lists, mint settings, reader grants and the escrowed keys `KeyRegistration` proves in place of `buildRingListWriteTransaction`, `prepareRingKeyRegistration`, `buildRingKeyRegistrationTransaction`, `registerRingKeyInstruction`, `grantReadAccessInstruction`, `revokeReadAccessInstruction` and the ring entry instructions and readers, and `fetchReaderGrant`, `fetchRingConfigs`, `fetchRingDelegate`, `fetchRingProgramConfig`, `decodeRingProgramConfig`, `ringConfigAddress`, `RingProgramConfig` and `RingKeyRegistryReader` are gone, and `ringRole` answers `admin`, `policy`, `pauser` or `cosignAdmin` in place of `authority` → sign each record update and send it to Ring RPC, and read the delegate and the role keys from `fetchRingPolicy`.
- A ring's policy lives in its `RingPolicy` account, which `fetchRingPolicy` reads, `RuleTable` holds rules only, each mint's thresholds, outflow limits, withdrawal threshold and `AssetLimits` live in the mint's record as a `MintPolicy` keyed by `memberOfAsset(mint)`, transfers, deposits and merges name each root by its storage revision from the policy's mirrors, and `RingPolicyConfig`, `fetchRingPolicyConfig`, `RING_POLICY_VERSION` and the spend record, spend window and velocity surface are gone → write mint settings with `AuthorityRecordUpdate.withMintPolicy`, send `syncRootsInstruction` after `initializeRingInstruction`, a records rebind, a policy write that names a new curator and any storage update Ring RPC does not send, and re-pin each policy under `POLICY_TABLE_VERSION`.
- A wallet transfers, exits, enters, withdraws, deposits and merges through the Ring RPC relay with `prepareRingTransferRelay`, `prepareRingExitRelay`, `prepareRingEntryRelay`, `prepareRingWithdrawalRelay`, `PreparedRingDeposit.prepareRelay` and `prepareRingMergeRelay`, `buildRingTransferTransaction`, `buildRingExitTransaction`, `buildRingEntryTransaction`, `buildRingWithdrawalTransaction`, `buildRingDepositTransaction`, `buildRingMergeTransaction` and their submission builders are gone, `CustomRingTransfer`, `AnonymousTransfer` and `DelegateTransfer` replace `proveCustomRingTransfer`, `proveCustomRingDelegateTransfer` and `encryptCustomRingTransfer`, and a ring transfer past `TRANSACTION_SIZE_LIMIT` fails before any proof with `RING_TRANSFER_TOO_WIDE` → quote the relay with `RingRpc.getRelayQuote`, send the prepared relay to `RingRpc`, store an anonymous relay's `viewingHistory` before sending it, and split a payout that does not fit.
- The ring program, its accounts, proofs and outputs change format, transfers use instruction tags 43 and 44 and policy statement version 4, anonymous proofs bind the fee payer, the signing co-signer and the withdrawal destination, every merge carries a disclosure proof and takes at most `MAX_RING_MERGE_INPUTS` notes, every deposit proves its disclosure and on a policy ring each deposited asset against the asset rules, a ring output carries no data, and `setRingDepositAuditInstruction`, `fetchRingDepositAudit` and `RingDepositAudit` are gone → deploy this release's ring program, shielded-pool program, prover and pinned keys together, and expect no state, note or proof of an earlier ring to carry over.
- `auditRingTransaction` takes the `layout` the ring program verified from the `ConfirmedRingTransaction` that `TransactionOrigin.confirmedTransaction` returns, `AuditedRingTransaction` and `DecryptedRingTransaction` hold each slot once in `outputs` or `payloadErrors` with the hidden `sender` and `inputs` of an anonymous transfer, every signed `RingRpc` read takes a `RingReadTarget` of the cluster's genesis hash, the pinned service key and the ring in place of `ringProgramId`, `auditorKeyStatement`, `sealTransactionViewingSecret`, `openTransactionViewingSecret`, `openRingDisclosure` and `policyStatementHash` replace `auditorKeyAttestation`, `encryptTransactionViewingSecret`, `decryptTransactionViewingSecret`, `openAuditOutputDisclosure` and `policyPublicInputHash`, and `CachedTransactionOrigin` and `openRingEscrowedKeys` are gone → implement `confirmedTransaction` in a custom origin, read amounts, mints and owners from each opening's `utxo`, pass `escrowedKeys` to `auditRing` on a key-escrow ring, and pin the Ring RPC service key in each `RingReadTarget`.
- `RingErrorCode` renames `RING_KEY_REGISTRY_INVALID` and `RING_KEY_REGISTRY_MISSING` to `RING_MEMBER_REGISTRY_INVALID` and `RING_MEMBER_REGISTRY_MISSING`, adds `RING_REGISTRY_OUT_OF_SYNC`, `RING_REGISTRY_ROOT_CHANGED`, `RING_REGISTRY_PAGE_GAP` for the ring indexer's refusals and `RING_ROOT_NOT_MIRRORED` for an answer under a root the ring policy does not mirror, drops the spend record, spend window, velocity, deposit audit, policy config and read access codes, `RingRpc` reports a Ring RPC refusal as `RING_RPC_REFUSED` with its `RingRpcRefusal` name in `details.refusal` in place of `RING_RPC`, `fetchRingPolicy` fails with `RING_POLICY_MISSING` for a ring without its `RingPolicy` account and no call raises `RING_CONFIG_NOT_FOUND`, and `RingProgramError` names every ring program error code and drops `staleKeyRegistryRoot` → update exhaustive switches and error matches.
- `CompressedProof.toCustomRingProof()` is gone → call `customRingProof(proof)`
  with the prover's proof.
- `syncRingWallet` and `openRingNotes` open ring deposits, an audited one
  through the ring capsule, and `syncWallet` no longer does → sync ring
  wallets with `syncRingWallet` and drop `depositPayloadDecoder` from the
  `syncWallet` config.
- `RingRpc` now posts each JSON-RPC method beneath its base URL and accepts
  `RingRpcOptions.apiKey` or the URL's `api-key`, while `RingRpc.url` omits the key;
  service clients reject malformed or conflicting credentials during construction
  and preserve unrelated URL queries → forward method paths unchanged and supply
  the API key in one place.
- `openRingDepositOpening` requires a `ConfirmedDeposit` → take it from `ConfirmedDeposit.fromTransaction` or from the `deposits` of a confirmed deposit's `ConfirmedRingTransaction`.
- Custom-ring auditor envelopes use X25519 keys → create a new auditor configuration with `X25519Keypair`/`X25519PublicKey` before using this version.
- Ring deposit audit capsules carry a `RING_DEPOSIT_OPENING_LENGTH` (96) byte opening that also discloses the Ed25519 owner key of an anonymous ring deposit, `RingDepositOpening` takes it as `ownerKey` and `openRingDepositOpening` returns it → re-prove audited deposits with this version, a capsule sealed by an earlier version no longer decodes.

Added

- `RingRpc.getRelayQuote`, `prepareRelay`, `submitRelay` and `getRelayStatus` call the Ring RPC relay, which proves, pays and sends a wallet's prepared operation and creates the associated token account a relayed SPL withdrawal pays, `CustomRingTransfer.withInProofOwner(signer)` has the sender sign inside the shielded pool's proof so any fee payer sends a confidential transfer, and `RingRpc.prepareAuthorityTransfer` lets the delegate of a `confidentialEscrow` ring move a wallet's notes to recipients in the same ring.
- `RingRpc.getRecordUpdateStatus` follows a record update, `WalletRecordUpdate.awaitingAuthority(lists)` makes an enrolment that `RingRpc.getPendingEnrolments` pages and `RingRpc.refuseEnrolment` refuses for the policy authority, `RingRpc.setReaderGrant` stores a reader grant on a Ring RPC that keeps grants in its database, `RingRpc` fails with `RING_RPC_RATE_LIMITED` past the rate of its client or signing key, and `signRecordUpdate`, `verifyRecordUpdate`, `encodeStorageWrites` and `decodeStorageWrites` in `@heliuslabs/zolana-custom-rings/advanced` match the Rust SDK byte for byte.
- `AnonymousSync` finds an anonymous wallet's payments by view tag and lists its own as `SentPayment` rows, `ViewingHistory` keeps its tag counters and counterparties per viewing key, a `RingPaymentRequest` carries the request tag `ViewingHistory.requestTag` mints, `syncRingWallet` follows the spends of the notes it opens, `auditRingMerges`, `RingRpc.getAuditedMerges` and `RingRpc.getAuditedUtxos` open a ring's merges and UTXOs for a reader, and `CustomRingClient.checkRingProvingKeys()` returns the prover's key catalog, refusing a digest other than `RING_PROVING_KEY_SHA256S` pins with `CLIENT_PROVER_PROVING_KEYS_MISMATCH` and a missing ring key with `RING_PROVING_KEYS_MISSING`.
- `RingRpc.createRecordsRegistry` has Ring RPC create a ring's records under its storage authority and returns the `RecordsRegistry` to bind at `initializeRingInstruction`, signed by `signCreateRecordsRegistryRequest` as the policy authority or, before `initializeRingInstruction`, the upgrade authority, `RingRpcRefusal.recordsNotCreated` names a creation that did not land, and a returned `registry` that is not the ring's records under its `storageAuthority` fails with `RING_RPC`.

Fixed

- A hand-built `RuleTable` skipped the checks `decodeRuleTable` runs, now policy writes, `policyTableProofInputs` and its hash rerun `buildRuleTable` and refuse such a table with `RING_RULE_TABLE_INVALID`.
- `RingRpc` accepted an incomplete, repeated or oversized signed audit page and `RpcTransactionOrigin` refused a confirmed transaction that loads an address lookup table, which stopped `auditRing`, now such a page fails, a last page naming neither a next cursor nor a scan frontier fails with `RING_SCAN_FRONTIER_MISSING`, and `confirmedInstructionGroups` reads the loaded accounts after the static keys.
