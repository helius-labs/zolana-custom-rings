## Measurement scope

The minimum and maximum output capacities are measured for confidential audit. Anonymous audit measures the minimum shape, maximum input class at 2 outputs, and maximum output capacity at input class 1. There is no 32-input/16-output key. Member policy measures 2 outputs without withdrawals, 16 outputs without withdrawals, and 16 outputs with the maximum compiled withdrawal capacity, for own and curator registries.

Input class and output/withdrawal capacities describe compiled circuits; they do not guarantee a transaction fits Solana's limits. Anonymous input classes 4, 32 and 1 use 2, 32 and 1 live inputs respectively. Anonymous fixtures include a pair payment and change. The 16-output confidential fixture has 15 live outputs; the 16-output anonymous fixture has 7; 16-output policy fixtures have 10. Remaining output slots are padding. Policy fixtures exercise list rules, escrow and an outflow cap. Maximum withdrawal fixtures fill their withdrawal capacity.

Timing includes assignment construction, witness encoding, constraint solving and Groth16 proving through the CPU `ExtensionProof.Prove` path. Key loading, fixture construction, JSON decoding, warm-up and proof verification are outside the timer. Warm-up and final proofs are verified. These are independent circuits; SPP proofs, network requests and transaction submission are excluded. Rows cannot be summed to claim a measured complete transfer or a comparison against Zolana's combined circuit.
